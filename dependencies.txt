Starship Thingy Mabobs and Such 0.5.3 - dependencies
=====================================================

For Kerbal Space Program 1.12.5. The version after each mod is the one this release was built and checked with;
newer versions should work. Install them first, then copy GameData/StarshipThingyMabobsAndSuch from this zip into your
GameData folder.


REQUIRED
--------
Starship Expansion Project (SEP)        3.1.0      https://github.com/Kari1407/Starship-Expansion-Project
    The Raptor engines, the Block 3 ship, nosecones and cargo section that most parts build on or patch.
    Install SEP with its own dependencies (CKAN does this for you).
ModuleManager                           4.2.3
B9PartSwitch                            2.21.0
Community Resource Pack                 (comes with SEP)
    Liquid methane for the ITS tanks.


NEEDED FOR SOME PARTS
---------------------
Breaking Ground (Squad expansion)
    The cargo winch, crane frame, both HLS elevators, and the cargo floors and tray are robotic parts.
Waterfall                               0.11.0     (comes with SEP)
    Engine plumes for the ITS engine rings and the HLS landing thrusters.
SimpleAdjustableFairings                1.12.0
    The Block 3 Payload Plate and Fairing.
Kerbal Reusability Expansion            2.9.3
    The ITS grid fins (deployable aero surfaces).
Starship Launch Expansion (SLE)         0.5.0
    The Crew Access Arm and Ship Quick Disconnect Arm attach to SLE's launch tower.
StagedAnimation
    The tower arms retract when staged.
HabTechProps                            0.2.0
    Seats, displays, lockers and galley in every crew interior. Without it the cabins and crew seats still work,
    but those props are missing.


OPTIONAL
--------
FreeIva                                 0.2.20.2   https://spacedock.info/mod/3151/FreeIva
    Walk around inside, use the hatches and the airlock. The cabins follow the Docking door switch.

Avionics cockpit - needs all four of these; adds a Cockpit switch (Classic / Avionics) to every crew cabin:
    FreeIva                             0.2.20.2   (above)
    RasterPropMonitor                   1.1.0.1    https://github.com/FirstPersonKSP/RasterPropMonitor/releases
                                                   (RasterPropMonitor-Core is enough)
    ASET Consolidated Props Pack        2.0.7      https://spacedock.info/mod/3240/ASET%20Consolidated%20Props%20Pack
    ASET Consolidated Avionics Pack     3.0.1      https://spacedock.info/mod/3239/ASET%20Consolidated%20Avionics%20Pack
  and, for more of its pages and buttons:
    MechJeb2                                       Autopilot pages and buttons (SmartASS, manoeuvre planner, ascent,
                                                   rendezvous, docking and landing autopilots).
    SCANsat                             21.1       Map pages.
    VesselView                                     Vessel viewer page.
    Docking Port Alignment Indicator               Docking alignment page.
    Astrogator                                     Transfer planner page.
    Chatterer                                      Radio panel.
    ALCOR Adopted                       1.0.1      Not needed; the cockpit is laid out after ALCOR's.

Hull cameras - RasterPropMonitor or Hullcam VDS (B9PartSwitch for the Camera pods switch): eight camera views on the
    Block 3 ship and the HLS (landing, engine bay, flaps, hull, nose). On SEP's parts they use SEP's own cameras where it
    has them. With RasterPropMonitor they show on the cockpit's camera monitors.

CryoTanks                               1.6.7      The storage depots keep their methane cold (less boil-off).
Community Category Kit                             All parts under "Starship Thingy Mabobs" in the editor's
                                                   Filter by Function list.
Connected Living Space                             Crew transfer between the ITS crew section and the ship.
Community Tech Tree                                ITS parts placed in its tech nodes.
USI Life Support, TAC Life Support or Snacks       Life support supplies in the ITS crew section, the HLS crew
                                                   sections and the Block 3 crew noses.


NOT SUPPORTED
-------------
Realism Overhaul: the SEP patches in this mod skip themselves under RO.

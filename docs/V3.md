# V3 ship

Add-ons for the V3 ship in Kari's [Starship Expansion Project](https://github.com/Kari1407/Starship-Expansion-Project). Needs SEP and B9PartSwitch, plus Breaking Ground for the winch's pistons. Nothing in SEP's own folder is changed.

## Expendable config for the V3 body

`SEP_26_SHIP_CORE` gains a **Recovery Hardware** switch in the VAB. Expendable removes the tiles, ablative strip, aerocovers, flap pipes and flap nodes, swaps in bare-steel masks, and drops skin temperature to 1700 K.

Mass is 23.82 t against 25 t, from SEP's own V1 ratio: its expendable body saves 1 t for 187.42 m2 of tiles and aerocovers (5.336 kg/m2), and V3 removes 220.60 m2. Only the body changes; the V3 nose and cargo section keep their tiles.

## Block 3 crew nosecones

Two new parts in the Pods tab, each a copy of SEP's Block 3 nosecone (`SEP_26_SHIP_NOSECONE`) made at load time from your SEP install. They keep SEP's model, flap nodes, RCS and Ablative switch, and add our window kit as a second model: ITS-style frames and glass with cabin lights (Light action group). Nothing of SEP's is shipped, and SEP's own nosecone is unchanged.

| Part | Windows | Crew | Mass |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Block 3 Crew Nosecone** (`STMS_B3_NoseCrew`) | The ITS big lattice window, and 29 small ones in three rows below it | 12 | SEP's + 2 t |
| **Block 3 Crew-Cargo Nosecone** (`STMS_B3_NoseCrewCargo`) | The ITS big lattice window, and one row of 9 small ones | 4 | SEP's + 1 t |

The big window is 4.2 m wide along the skin at its straight lower edge (z 2.65 in the nose's frame) and arches up to z 4.6, about 2 m. The rows' count per nose is one number, `NOSE_SMALL_COUNT` in `v3/scripts/layout.py`.

The crew-cargo nose goes on SEP's Block 3 cargo section, which carries the payload. Both noses lose SEP's `ModuleCargoBay`: a cabin holds no cargo, and that bay has no doors, so it was only an occlusion volume that could reach parts at the top of the section below. The interior is the stock Mk3 cabin as a placeholder, and EVA uses SEP's hatch at the bottom of the leeward barrel.

Fit: the windows sit on the leeward bare steel only (azimuth 0, Unity -Z; the tiles face +Z), clear of the tiles with either Ablative subtype, the two small tile patches at mid-height, the cameras and the flap aerocovers. Every frame and rib is bent onto the 48 facets of SEP's shell and stands 14-24 mm proud. The surface comes from SEP's ring radii, stored as numbers in `v3/scripts/layout.py`, and `v3/scripts/check_nose.py` tests the numbers and the kits against SEP's model on every build where SEP is installed. Crew numbers: the shell holds 184 m3, so 12 kerbals get 15 m3 each; the crew-cargo cabin is the 50 m3 top deck.

## Cargo winch

1. Attach **Cargo Winch Boom** to the `winch` node on the ceiling of `SEP_26_SHIP_CARGO`, and roll it so the rails point out of the door.
2. Attach **Cargo Winch Hoist** under the boom's crossbeam, then **Cargo Winch Platform** under the hoist.
3. Put cargo on the deck. In flight: open the doors, take the boom to -4.4, then lower the hoist toward -29.1. At 0 the platform hangs right under the winches, with 2.4 m clear above the deck under the hoist crossbeam; the deck reaches the bay floor at about -3. Reverse it to bring the platform back, and retract fully before closing the doors.

Turn autostrut off on the winch parts and anything on the platform, or the pistons will lock up. The cables are a rigid piston under the skin, so the load doesn't swing.
